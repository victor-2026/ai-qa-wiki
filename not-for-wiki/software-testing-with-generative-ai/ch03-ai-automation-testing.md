# Artificial Intelligence, Automation, and Testing

**Source**: Chapter 3, *Software Testing with Generative AI* (pages 66-90)

## 3.1 Why We Test

### Testing = Learning

Not just confirmation — testing helps us:
- Discover risks
- Find edge cases
- Understand behavior
- Inform stakeholders

### Traditional vs Broader View

| Traditional | Broader |
|------------|--------|
| Confirm works | Learn about product |
| Check specs | Discover unknowns |

---

## 3.2 AI's Role

### AI vs Traditional Automation

| Aspect | Traditional | AI-Augmented |
|--------|-------------|--------------|
| Flexibility | Fixed rules | Learns patterns |
| Adaptation | Static | Dynamic |

### AI Helps In

1. Test generation from requirements
2. Test data creation
3. Test oracles identification
4. Risk discovery
