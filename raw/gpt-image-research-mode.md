Source: Twitter/X thread

## Key Point

GPT Image 2 can research topics in the web before generating images. Instead of detailed description, just name the subject + style.

## Example

Old: "Draw VAZ-2101 with chrome bumpers, yellow headlights..."
New: "research VAZ-2101 'Zhiguli', then draw as studio photo"

Model finds details about the car and generates accurately.
