# The Verge — Rabbit's New AI Agent Doesn't Need an R1 to Run (2026-09-22)

**Author:** Emma Roth (The Verge)
**Source:** https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/999094/rabbit-ai-agent-os3
**Context:** The Verge news on Rabbit OS3 "agentic operating system". First reported by Wired (Jesse Lyu profile). Digest 23.09 candidate.

---
Rabbit (company behind the underwhelming R1 device) launches a standalone AI agent that you don't need its hardware to use. Its new **OS3** "agentic operating system" runs in the cloud but operates locally across Windows, Mac, and Linux devices.

Details:
- Add up to **five devices** to one account, along with preferred AI models.
- OS3 automatically determines the devices, files, apps, and AI models it needs to complete a task.
- Access via dedicated desktop site (os3.rabbit.tech), paired messaging app (Telegram, iMessage), or on the Rabbit R1 device.

Rabbit founder Jesse Lyu: startup has stopped manufacturing R1 devices but isn't giving up on AI hardware — focusing on a new vibe-coding "cyberdeck" that will run OS3 when it launches within "months".

Privacy: OS3 won't store, copy, use, or sell your data; will still keep your chats and memories stemming from them on its servers. Other AI providers linked to OS3 handle data under their own privacy rules.

QA relevance (digest): cross-platform agent (cloud + local on Windows/macOS/Linux) → hybrid deployment scenarios require testing; device/app/task auto-selection = test surface.
