## Core thesis (verbatim substance)

- Problem: regulations/standards/customer/supply-chain obligations explode (NIS2 management accountability, breach costs ~$4.44M avg); controls × risks × assets × services × compliance interrelations unmanageable by human navigation taxonomies.
- Proposal: ontology extends ESARIS taxonomy with machine-readable definitions + explicit relationships; connected knowledge model for humans AND machines.
- Payoff: multi-compliance (one architecture description mapped to many frameworks simultaneously) → traceability, consistency, less duplication; plus automated compliance analysis, decision support, AI-assisted governance, structured reasoning, Digital Twin futures.
- Roadmap: machine-readable standards → relationship modeling + user tools → operational adoption; orgs extend shared ontology locally, interoperate with partners.
- Collaboration thesis: ontology value = industry consensus (concepts, terminology, responsibilities), not technology; ZOIS positioned as facilitator.
- QA-of-ontology (§7.5-adjacent): quality assurance beyond technical validation - expert reviews, consistency checks, relationship verification, periodic reality-fit assessments; automated validation + stakeholder review combined.
