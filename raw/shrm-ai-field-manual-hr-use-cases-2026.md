## Four risk/cost quadrants (HR words for our tiers)

- **Low-Hanging Fruit** (low risk / low cost): mainstream tools, minor remediable mistakes. Entry points.
- **Experimental** (HIGH risk / low cost): easy to implement, compliance/strategic blunders hard to redress. SHRM advice VERBATIM: confine to controlled test environments to study future applications without undue risk. = sandbox doctrine from demand side.
- **Strategic Bets** (low risk / high cost): specialized tools, heavy human involvement, long adoption. Invest now to position.
- **Monitor** (high/high): technically possible, logistically hard, significant risk. Wait for tooling/best practices to mature.

## Four HR-X dimensions (16 practice areas)

- Functional Acceleration (HR strategy, HR tech); Market Discernment (recruiting, total rewards); Organizational Impact (compliance, C-suite/board, future of work, org design/change); Talent Optimization (EX, I&D, labor relations, leadership dev, L&D, performance, talent analytics, talent management).
