# Lee Robinson - Job Search Tips

Source: Twitter/X thread by Lee Robinson (@leerob)
Date: 2026-05-14
URL: https://twitter.com/leerob

## Context

Lee Robinson — VP Product at Cursor (AI code editor). Previously Head of Developer Experience at Vercel (Next.js creators). Has reviewed thousands of developer resumes.

## Key Points

1. One-page resume, or link to portfolio
2. Personal website differentiates from 90%
3. Clean your X/Twitter before applying
4. GitHub without MySpace-style badges
5. Customize resume per company type
6. Mention AI/agents in resume
7. Take LinkedIn seriously
8. Show unique interests/tastes
9. Don't use AI to write CL/CV
10. No photos in resume
11. Quality over quantity — 3 good projects vs 27 AI-slop
