## Thesis (author's)

- Toll-booth metaphor: added a lane, kept one window; cars faster, line longer, booth person = bottleneck.
- "We automated the typing. The thing that was never the slow part."
- Fix: questions BEFORE the prompt; after the PR is too late. Second person in booth won't help.
- Acceptance criteria as binary/done-definition (his long-standing doctrine: AC subjective garbage → binary commands).
