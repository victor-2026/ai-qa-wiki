# The Perfect Job For Me — VP of AI Quality Engineering (Satisfice, 2026-09-22)

**Author:** James Bach
**Source:** https://www.satisfice.com/blog/archives/488204
**Context:** Satisfice blog. 22 Sep 2026. Categories: AI and Testing, Management, Risk Analysis, RST, Testing Culture. Job-proposal essay.

---
Every large company must learn to safely apply AI or it will not be able to compete — regardless of whether AI improves, plateaus, or the bubble pops. **Safety and success with AI do not happen without careful governance.** What you *can* do without careful leadership: "spend millions of dollars on tokens with no clear idea what you got and did not get for all that money."

**Proposed role: VP of AI Governance / VP of AI Quality Engineering**, with a small team of analysts who track AI trends and experiment with methods of using and controlling AI, in collaboration with IT and R&D.

**What the AIQE team would do:** experiment with AI + follow outside research; monitor and orchestrate practical experimentation within IT/R&D to identify and manage opportunities and risks; monitor AI usage company-wide and report to top management on ongoing risks, benefits, costs; track compliance and manage communication with regulatory authorities when government regulation arrives. Small companies can "just try things willy-nilly"; large companies cannot lightly take on large risks — "a small measure of bureaucracy creates safety."

**Why Bach for it:** prestige in critical thinking about software (testing); decades of reporting technical risk truthfully — "whoever you hire should have a public reputation for speaking truth to power"; teaching/evangelizing responsible behavior; assertiveness (telling people what they don't want to hear); recent experimentation with testing AI and using AI in testing; co-author of the Principles of Responsible Work (grounded in research about AI-related hazards). "We are technologists who are also humanists."

**On the alternative:** prestigious AI safety researchers and softer-conventional testing leaders exist; he'd be "a bold choice."
