A couple of months ago, I started thinking about how to keep communication within my family truly private.  
  
That thought turned into one of my pet projects - Family.Connect.  
  
And now it has reached its first public version. 🚀  
  
Family.Connect is a self-hosted family messenger where you can keep your family conversations and data on your own server.  
  
It supports family and private chats, photos and videos, files, voice messages, location sharing, polls, a family board, birthdays, and voice/video calls - with iOS, macOS and Android clients.  
  
Maybe this approach can be interesting for other families who also believe that family communication should stay within the family.  
  
If you share this vision, I’d love to hear your thoughts.
