# Source: Keith Klain — "The Flurry of Constraints" (Quality Remarks)

**URL:** https://qualityremarks.com/the-flurry-of-constraints/ · **Published:** 24.09.2026
**Fetched:** 2026-09-29 (webfetch, markdown). Context: AGNTCon + MCPCon Europe, Amsterdam (via Angie Jones / AAIF, 2.5 days, protocols/standards/orchestration/evaluation).

---

- Familiar words served "with equal confidence and roughly the same relationship to what they were supposed to mean" (Cajun popcorn shrimp in mayo-popcorn bowl as the culinary equivalent).
- Shared tone: inevitability + unwarranted urgency ("genie is out of the bottle", "no going back", "if we don't do it, somebody else will", lose-the-AI-race warnings). Conversations felt ahistorical (decades of democratization promises → surveillance, algorithmic discrimination, fragmentation, environmental damage, concentration).
- Governance framed as a constraint on growth strategy; the event's central contradiction, quoted: **"Governance should never slow innovation. Governance should make it scalable."** Klain: "Sorry, governance is not another production capability for getting systems deployed faster. Its purpose is to impose boundaries to protect people and prevent organizations from taking risks when somebody else will have to live with the consequences."
- **Human judgment as the binding constraint:** "According to the governance models I saw presented, meaningful human judgment is the main assurance constraint in an agentic system." Theory of Constraints corollary: production should be subordinated to competent-review capacity. Industry does the opposite: accelerates code/decisions/actions while automating the reviewer into irrelevance.
- EU AI Act talk (brilliant) + Q&A: Art. 14 requires effective human oversight for high-risk systems; speaker "shrugged and said they didn't know how that would be managed in reality" — "probably the most honest answer to a governance question I heard all week."
- Europe 2031 / AI Omnibus: safety regulation permanently conditional on not inconveniencing industry arrives too late by construction.
- **Cook #3:** "Overt catastrophic failure occurs when small, apparently innocuous failures join to create opportunity for a systemic accident" (Richard Cook, "How Complex Systems Fail"). Failure mode = thousands of individually defensible decisions by well-meaning people, not a rogue superintelligence.
- **No natural race:** "There is no naturally occurring AI race. Organizations choose what to fund, engineers choose what to build, executives choose what they deploy, and governments choose what to permit. Presenting those choices as unstoppable progress conveniently removes responsibility from the people making them."
- Closer: "Sometimes constraints on those ambitions are the only things protecting us from ourselves."
