# This Fact About KPIs May Surprise You (Satisfice, 2026-08-23)

**Author:** James Bach
**Source:** https://www.satisfice.com/blog/archives/488189
**Context:** Satisfice blog. KPIs, metrics skepticism. Categories: Metrics, Management, Testing Culture.

---
Meeting a young quality manager, all enthused about her KPI dashboard: key numbers, budgets, defect counts, and so on. In a "smug, senior mood" Bach pointed to the graph and said "None of these things are a measurement of quality." The manager: "It's a measurement of performance!" — whereupon Bach: "**Performance is not quality.** Choose the metric that describes what you actually want.**"**

The problem with minimizing metrics by fiat: if you measure the wrong thing, you will commit terrible brutalities against quality. "KPI" nowadays is a swear word to craftsmen. Metrics are enablers of management, not a substitute for judgment: quality is largely a matter of judgment, not measurement. Passion for measurement can degenerate into a passion ONLY for measurement.

Closing: "Stop degrading the word 'quality'." When you set up or report a KPI dashboard, ask: is this measuring quality, or performance? Are you treating measurement as an alternative to judgment — or as a tool that serves it?
