by Ruslan Desyatnikov
Over the past 28 years, I have interviewed thousands of QA professionals. The questions I asked five years ago are not the same ones I ask today. Why? Because AI has fundamentally changed what it means to be an effective tester.  
  
Today, almost every candidate says they use ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude, Gemini, or another AI tool. That does NOT differentiate them. What I want to understand is how they think when AI is part of the equation. That's why I assembled a list of AI-focused interview questions I now use for Manual QA Professionals interviews.  
  
**AI & Critical Thinking**  
1. Tell me about a time AI confidently gave you the wrong answer. How did you recognize it?  
2. What AI-generated output would you never accept without manual review and why?  
3. How do you determine whether AI-generated test cases are complete?  
4. Can you describe a situation where you intentionally ignored AI's recommendation?  
5. How do you know when AI has missed an important business risk?  
  
**AI & Risk Analysis**  
6. AI generated 500 test cases. How would you determine whether they are sufficient?  
7. What types of defects do you think AI is least likely to identify?  
8. How would you validate that AI-generated tests provide meaningful risk coverage rather than just high test coverage?  
9. How would you prioritize AI-generated test cases?  
10. What business risks do you believe AI consistently underestimates?  
  
**AI & Requirements**  
11. You upload a user story into ChatGPT and receive 200 test cases. What's your next step?  
12. How do you verify that AI correctly interpreted ambiguous requirements?  
13. How would you use AI to improve poor requirements before creating test cases?  
14. Can AI identify missing requirements? If yes, how would you validate its suggestions?  
  
**AI & Decision Making**  
15. When would you completely ignore AI-generated output?  
16. If AI and your experience disagree, what do you do?  
17. How much confidence do you place in AI-generated answers? Why?  
18. What is AI's biggest weakness in software testing?  
  
**AI & Productivity**  
19. Besides generating test cases, how has AI genuinely made you a better tester?  
20. What testing activities have you stopped doing manually because of AI?  
21. What activities would you never delegate entirely to AI?  
22. Has AI made you more effective, or simply faster? Give an example.  
  
**AI & Leadership**  
23. How would you prevent junior testers from becoming overdependent on AI?  
24. If your team blindly trusted AI, what risks would concern you most?  
25. How would you measure whether AI actually improved testing quality?  
26. What AI governance practices would you introduce into a QA organization?  
  
**AI Evaluation**  
27. How would you test an AI agent before allowing it to generate production test cases?  
28. What metrics would you use to evaluate AI-generated test cases?  
29. How would you detect hallucinations in AI-generated testing artifacts?  
30. How would you compare two AI models for test design quality?
