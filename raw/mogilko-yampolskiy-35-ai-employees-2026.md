## LinkedIn Post (full text)

Roman Yampolskiy has 35 AI employees working for him for free.

He said 35 is exactly how many people you used to need to launch a company. Lawyer, accountant, designer, developer, assistant, researcher. Today one person does the same work with 35 AI agents, and it costs nothing.

We've heard the same thing from Gary Vaynerchuk, Amjad Masad (founder of Replit) and others on the show.

From what I see running my own company, this doesn't mean cut headcount. My team is 35 people and I'm planning to hire more. If 35 people are already driving 2x revenue, the smarter move is to hire more and get to 5x.

I asked Roman if it's still worth building a personal brand now. He pointed to Twitter: no one built a clone that took the market, because buyers weren't paying for the code. They paid 44 billion for the network of users and their habit of coming back.

Three things are working for you right now:

1. Tools got cheap and nearly unlimited. What used to take a team and a budget, one person now does with AI.

2. Networks cannot be copied. Your audience, their trust, the habit of opening your channel: AI doesn't produce this.

3. The first mover with a network wins. Once you become "the one" in your niche, even a competitor with a better product cannot push you out.

What you can do this week:

• Build your own team of 2–3 agents. AI helpers for narrow tasks: copy, research, design, finance, calendar.

• Pick one platform where you build recognition, so in six months people know you by name. That's what stays with you in any scenario.
