# Beyond Zero — Google's Experimentation Culture

> Google (2026). Author: Nicole Pearson. URL: https://research.google/blog/beyond-zero-the-culture-of-experimentation-at-google/

## Core Thesis
> "A culture of experimentation is the foundation of innovation."

## Five Key Components
1. **Psychological Safety** — people must feel safe to propose, fail, and iterate
2. **Data-Driven Decisions** — rely on empirical evidence over intuition
3. **Collaborative Environment** — cross-functional teamwork accelerates discovery
4. **Continuous Learning** — every experiment, success or failure, generates knowledge
5. **Leadership Support** — leaders must model and reward experimentation

## Metrics Framework
Google emphasizes measuring experimentation velocity:
- Experiments per engineer per quarter
- Time from idea to first experiment
- Percentage of features validated through experimentation
- Failure-to-success ratio (healthy range indicates ambition)

## Connection to Testing
Experimentation culture IS testing culture:
- **Hypothesis** = test case
- **Experiment** = test execution
- **Metrics** = assertions
- **Learning** = test result analysis

## Key Insight
> "You can't innovate without being willing to fail."

Organizations that punish failure kill experimentation. Organizations that learn from failure accelerate.
