## Nace AI Drex – Small Diffusion Decision Model (Decision Index #1, 2026‑09‑25)

### Summary  
Nace AI’s **Drex** is a compact diffusion‑based decision engine (≤ 6 B parameters) augmented with a proprietary RLAF (reinforcement‑learning‑from‑attributed‑feedback) layer. In the vendor’s “Decision Index 0.2” benchmark it scores **51.73** versus Jev 1.13.0’s **51.67**, winning 22 of 39 test cases while using only **≈70 tokens per decision** (≈5 × fewer than Jev). Drex can be deployed self‑hosted (single accelerator), managed, or hybrid, and ships with customer‑tuned weight files that act as a calibration‑by‑training mechanism. The model supports three canonical question formats—multiple‑choice, yes/no, and scalar rating—and returns a probability distribution that can be thresholded or routed to a human reviewer.
