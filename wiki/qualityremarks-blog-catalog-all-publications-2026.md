## Key Strengths & Gaps (for QA/QE)

**Strengths:**
- Authoritative voice on **verification asymmetry** (AI generates faster than can be credibly evaluated) — complements Zalando's CCN inflection and Martin Fowler's data-contracts/barrier.
- **Testing mindset ≠ gatekeeping** — epistemic position argument (different assumptions/risk models) directly maps to LLM-as-judge evaluator independence (generator vs judge).
- **Great Liberation** trilogy provides management accountability lens (what AI changes about creation/testing/expertise) — useful for Article 27 (guided QA engineer) and governance.

**Gaps / Watch-outs:**
- Boutique blog (single author, 170 posts) — not large-scale empirical like Kiro 1.5M convos or Zalando 250 teams; more essay/position than harness code.
- AI Act content is awareness-level, not implementation harness (vs Zalando's pipeline or Martin Fowler's contract YAML).
