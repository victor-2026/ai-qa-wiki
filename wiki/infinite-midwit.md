# Infinite Midwit
## Summary
The concept of Infinite Midwit refers to the limitations of Large Language Models (LLMs) in solving subjective problems. Despite their ability to process vast amounts of data and provide objective intelligence, LLMs struggle with tasks that require subjective judgment, creativity, or emotional understanding. This limitation is attributed to the inherent difference between objective and subjective intelligence, with the latter being impossible to replicate through scaling or increased computational power.

## Key Concepts
* **Objective Intelligence**: The ability of LLMs to solve bounded problems with verifiable solutions, such as grammar and syntax checks.
* **Subjective Intelligence**: The capacity for subjective judgment, creativity, and emotional understanding, which is currently beyond the capabilities of LLMs.
* **The Wall**: The fundamental limitation of LLMs in overcoming subjective intelligence, which cannot be bridged by scaling or increased computational power.
* **Bottleneck Blindness**: The phenomenon where removing one bottleneck (e.g., cheap research) reveals another (e.g., motivation, ideas, direction), highlighting the importance of human judgment in subjective tasks.

## Practical Applications
* **Testing AI**: Distinguish between objective and subjective requirements when testing AI systems to ensure effective evaluation.
* **AI Writing Tests**: Recognize that while grammar and syntax can be checked, "alive writing" that engages the reader requires subjective judgment.
* **Bottleneck Thinking**: Identify the actual bottlenecks limiting quality, rather than simply adding more testing or computational power.
* **Human Judgment**: Acknowledge the continued need for human judgment in subjective tasks, such as evaluating the quality or relevance of AI-generated content.

## Related Topics
* **Comprehension Debt**: The issue of LLMs generating code that is difficult for humans to understand, highlighting the need for transparency and explainability in AI systems.
* **RAG Evaluation**: The use of LLMs as judges in evaluation tasks, which is still subjective and requires human oversight to ensure fairness and accuracy.
* **AI Limitations**: The ongoing research into the limitations of AI systems, including their inability to replicate human-like subjective intelligence, creativity, and emotional understanding.
