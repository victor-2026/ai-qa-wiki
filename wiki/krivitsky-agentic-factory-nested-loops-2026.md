## Alexey Krivitsky — Agentic Factory: Nested Loops (Coding / Feature / Impact)

**Summary**
Krivitsky frames agentic AI as three **nested** work-and-learning loops, each wrapping the next: (1) **Coding Loop** (Red→Green→Refactor) optimizes implementation correctness; (2) **Feature Loop** (Refine→Specify→Verify) adds behavioral usefulness via executable specs; (3) **Impact Loop** (Opportunity→Hypothesis→Impact) redirects the system toward real-world outcomes. Most tools stop at Level 2. The outer loop is the one that matters - and it requires humans. "A Feature Factory is not the final stage; it is only the middle layer."

## Our analysis (for Victor)

1. **Three nested loops = a clean scaffold for the Quality Operating Model.** Coding (agent checks) → Feature (agent+eval verifies) → Impact (human owns outcome). This is literally Article 24/27's layered model, stated by an org-topologies authority. Use as the visual spine for Article 27: the guided QA engineer operates the outer two loops, not the inner one.

2. **"Most tools stop at Level 2" = the exact gap Victor exploits.** Krivitsky says current agentic dev systems are Feature Factories; the Impact Loop (outcome, risk, accountability) still needs humans. That is Victor's entire value prop: AI does coding + features, QA owns the impact/outcome gate. Direct citation for Article 27 + Article 21.

3. **Ferrari Trap = Article 20 in one line.** "A faster engine doesn't help if pointed at a wall" = an AI that finds 5 bugs, 4 imaginary, fast. Speed without verification is irrelevance. Pair with Ng's "agent worked an hour unchecked" and Bach's "magic testing box."

4. **Outer loop constrains inner = Bolton's neck.** Krivitsky: outer loops "constrain and guide" inner loops so local optimization serves broader goals. Bolton: bottles have necks to regulate output. Same metaphor, different words - the human gate is the outer-loop constraint.

5. **Impact Loop ≈ Conway's business-outcome owner.** Article 21: "Accountability should follow the business outcome." Krivitsky's Impact Loop is the mechanism - real-world signals reshape priorities. Cross-link the two; Krivitsky gives the loop model, Article 21 gives the QA operating model.
