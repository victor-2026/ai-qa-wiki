## Prachi Dahibhate — James Bach and Rapid Software Testing in the AI era

**Summary**
Prachi Dahibhate's "Testing Today" piece profiles James Bach and the Rapid Software Testing (RST) lineage (with Michael Bolton, Cem Kaner, Brian Marick, Bret Pettichord). The through-line is directly applicable to AI-assisted testing: Bach's "magic testing box" problem, the Testing-vs-Checking split, and Bolton's Productivity Paradox all map onto Victor's article series (16/20/26/27) and the Mutation Matrix method.

## Key Concepts (mapped to our work)

| Bach / RST idea | Quote / essence | Maps to |
|-----------------|----------------|---------|
| **Magic testing box** | A box reports bugs - "How do you know it did a good job?" If it reports none, are there none, or did it miss them? | Article 20 (5 bugs / 4 imaginary - false discovery); Article 26 (break the tool on purpose) |
| **Productivity Paradox** | Bolton: AI saves time, but responsible use costs time; rewarding visible speed rewards irresponsible use. Answer may be "test the right risks," not "test faster." | Article 16 (verification layer) - exact same term Bolton coined |
| **Testing vs Checking** | Machine does repeatable checks; testing = learning, exploration, judgment | Article 27 (guided QA engineer: agent checks, human tests/sets gates) |
| **"Extend, don't replace"** | Use AI to extend your thinking, not replace it; step-back prompting | Article 27 "manifesting" - human supplies intent, agent implements |
