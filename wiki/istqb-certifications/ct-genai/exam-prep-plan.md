# CT-GenAI Exam Preparation Plan

## Overview

- **Version:** v1.1 (Apr 2026)
- **Questions:** 40 (46 max points)
- **Pass:** 30/46 (65%)
- **Duration:** 60 min (75 min non-native)
- **Format:** Multiple choice
- **Prerequisite:** CTFL

## Materials

1. **Syllabus v1.1** — primary source
2. **Sample Exam A v1.1** — 40 questions, full format

## 5 Modules

| # | Module | Weight | Key Topics |
|---|--------|--------|------------|
| 1 | Introduction to GenAI for SW Testing | ~20% | LLM architecture, tokenization, embeddings, context windows, multimodal |
| 2 | Prompt Engineering | ~25% | Structure (role/context/instruction), chaining, few-shot, evaluation |
| 3 | Managing Risks | ~20% | Hallucinations, bias, privacy, security, environmental impact, regulations |
| 4 | LLM-Powered Solutions | ~20% | RAG, agents, fine-tuning, LLMOps |
| 5 | Deploying & Integrating | ~15% | Roadmap, shadow AI, cost estimation, change management |

## Study Strategy

### Phase 1: Syllabus Read (1 week)
- Read syllabus cover-to-cover
- Focus on learning objectives (LOs) — exam questions map to LOs
- Highlight K-level (K1=remember, K2=understand, K3=apply)

### Phase 2: Sample Exam (3 days)
- Take Sample Exam A closed-book
- Score and identify weak modules
- Review missed answers with rationale

### Phase 3: Deep-dive weak areas (1 week)
- For modules <70%: re-read syllabus section + hands-on exercises
- Focus on K3 (apply) questions — they require understanding, not memorization

### Phase 4: Final review (2 days)
- Re-take Sample Exam A
- Review ISTQB glossary terms
- Time management: 1.5 min per question

## Key v1.1 Focus Areas

Compared to v1.0, v1.1 emphasizes:
1. **LLM-powered agents** — understand agentic patterns, tool use, autonomous testing
2. **Evaluation metrics** — refined descriptions of accuracy, precision, recall, relevance
3. **Risk clarification** — clearer boundaries between hallucination vs reasoning error
4. **Updated hands-on** — more practical exercises, less theoretical
