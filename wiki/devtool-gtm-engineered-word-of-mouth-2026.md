# Engineered Word-of-Mouth: Dev-Tool Growth Playbook (TypeSafe AI Case, Sep 2026)

Case material: TypeSafe AI Jev launch (250K waitlist, 40K Discord, #1 HN, founder + hands-on posts 15–19.09.2026). Raw notes: `../raw/typesafe-jev-judgment-service-gates-2026.md`.

---

## 1. Access as currency

- Staged windows, not open doors. Scarcity + fairness narrative ("releases roll out in windows").
- Meme-gating (Watsche access for a meme), hypothesis invites ("send a hypothesis to test"). Filter cost ~zero, selects loud builders with audiences.
- Rule: access goes to people who will publish the result, not to everyone.

## 2. Bench as content format

- The ideal third-party post: N messages, medians, thresholds, honest misses on both sides (Jev 61/62 at 377ms; Sonnet 98/101 with 2 literal-pass misses).
- Company gets evidence it could never credibly publish itself. Numbers + admitted limits = trust.
- Template: baseline vs new, same workload, misses disclosed, verdict qualified ("nice tool, used for the thing it's built for").

## 3. Funnel width

- Discord (40K) + waitlist (250K) + hackathons (CoreWeave) + vendor reposts. Cadence ("every 5 minutes") is arithmetic: wide top × fraction who post.
- Events produce artifacts (winning rocket demo, benchmark claims) that feed back into the funnel.

## 4. Amplification loop

- Founder story (ex-OpenAI pedigree) → press (Forbes) → CEO tweets → aggregator posts → hands-on benches → company reposts benches. Each layer borrows credibility from the previous.
- Launch metrics post (30M views, #1 HN) is itself funnel fuel, not a report.

## 5. Honesty constraints (where it breaks)

- Works while: users real, numbers replicable, misses admitted, access criteria legible (meme/hypothesis).
- Breaks when: numbers drawn not measured, shills undisclosed, access sold quietly while framed as earned. Detection is fast in dev communities (repro runs).
- Our game differs (articles + open repo + pilots) but shares the constraint: third-party verifiability or nothing.

## 6. Applicability checklist (for our launches)

- [ ] Gated access with legible criteria (not paywall, not lottery).
- [ ] Bench template given to early users (workload, medians, misses required).
- [ ] Honest-miss norm modeled from the top (founder posts own limits first).
- [ ] Funnel wide enough for cadence (community surface + waitlist).
- [ ] No paid hands presented as organic (one exposure kills the loop).
