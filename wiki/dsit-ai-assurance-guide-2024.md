# UK DSIT Introduction to AI assurance (2024-02, gov.uk)

**Source:** https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/introduction-to-ai-assurance/introduction-to-ai-assurance (HTML; PDF 44pp). Via Keith Klain post (talk teaser).
**Status:** state guidance, principles-level (Feb 2024, pre-frontier-wave)

## Саммари

Assurance = measure + evaluate + communicate trustworthiness. Пять принципов (safety/security/robustness, transparency, fairness, accountability, contestability/redress). "No silver bullet": комбинация техник по контексту и риску, low-risk — меньше, high-risk — robust combo.

## Таксономия механизмов

Risk assessment → impact assessment → bias audit → compliance audit → conformity assessment → formal verification (+ NCSC cyber baseline). Подложка — стандарты: ISO/IEC 42001 (AI management), 23894, TR 24027, TS 12791. Экосистема: regulators, UKAS (аккредитация), BSI/NPL (стандарты), Turing (исследования), civil society, professional bodies (IAAA — кодекс аудиторов).

## Наши зацепы

1. **"Without standards we have advice, not assurance"** — evidence без agreed standard = совет. Мост к нашим pre-agreed rules (W4: кандидат в банк).
2. **Measure/evaluate/communicate** = evidence → verdict → publish. Один в один.
3. **UKAS conflict-of-interest** (не аккредитует тех, кого ревьюит) — прецедент "examiner can't be the author" на уровне института.
4. **Assurance как экономическая активность** (£4B cyber-эквивалент) — аргумент для W1 про рынок attestation.
