### Comprehension Debt
#### Summary
Comprehension debt refers to the hidden cost of relying on Large Language Models (LLMs) to develop software without fully understanding the underlying code. This debt accumulates when developers prioritize velocity over comprehension, leading to a lack of ownership, context rot, and weak reviews. To mitigate comprehension debt, teams can implement robust PR reviews, AI-augmented review, quick feedback loops, and ownership.

#### Key Concepts
* **Comprehension debt**: the hidden cost of prioritizing velocity over understanding in software development
* **Velocity vs. comprehension**: the trade-off between rapid development and thorough understanding of the code
* **LLM limitations**: the potential for LLMs to mask a lack of understanding and lead to comprehension debt
* **Code ownership**: the importance of developers taking responsibility for their code and being able to articulate their work

#### Practical Applications
* Implement robust PR reviews with conventional commits and stacked PRs
* Use AI-augmented review to outline module hierarchies, suggest refactors, and verify assumptions
* Establish quick feedback loops with automated validation of business logic and outcomes
* Encourage ownership by expecting team members to articulate their work and ship in reviewable chunks
* Track metrics such as review iteration rate, follow-up PRs per PR, and revert rate to monitor comprehension debt
