## Key Strengths & Gaps (for QA/QE automation engineer)

**Strengths:**
- Strongest agentic regression/selective testing library (delegation ladder, skip recall, impact analysis, KaneAI intent re-anchoring) — more concrete than Kiro/Autonoma on what to delegate vs audit.
- Agent testing as category (video agents, CLI, assurance, orchestration, verification agent) — rare depth vs Zalando/testRigor.
- Concrete numbers: Facebook Predictive Test Selection 2× cost, 99.9% faulty-change recall; TDAD 6.08%→1.82% with impact map; Stack Overflow 66%/45.2%.

**Gaps / Watch-outs:**
- Very large, product-led blog — signal/noise ~6% HIGH; many generic tutorial/how-to/emulator posts.
- Product overlap: KaneAI/Kane CLI/Agent Assurance/HyperExecute — similar concepts repeated across product updates.
- Illustrative 5-check demo (4300ms→2050ms) not scale benchmark — needs full-suite validation.
