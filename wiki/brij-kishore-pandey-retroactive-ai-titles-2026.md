## Brij Kishore Pandey - Stop Adding AI to Old Job Titles

**Summary**
AI Architect Brij Kishore Pandey (736K LinkedIn, 7h, 81 reactions) cites Stanford/NBER paper tracking 29.4M US profiles: nearly 1 in 5 rewrote a job they had already left, median edit >4 years later. Since ChatGPT, retroactive adds of AI/GPT/LLM are up 6x. A 2026 snapshot makes 2022 look 30% more AI-skilled than it was. At same time, remote and DEI language is being quietly deleted. Hiring managers are not fooled.

---

## Five Signals That Actually Signal AI Skill in 2026

1. You can explain a production failure you caused
2. You can talk about evals, not RAG demos
3. You know when NOT to use an agent
4. You can estimate cost before you ship
5. You can defend a design to security, legal, and finance

**Pandey's hiring heuristic:** `I would rather hire the engineer who killed a bad agent than the one who launched 12.` If last 5 posts are `10 tools you need`, you compete with everyone. If they show how a system broke and what you changed, you compete with almost nobody.

**Comment thread:** Petar Ivanov notes AI-driven tacked onto basic automation; Kenny W. on every PM becoming AI PM and governance theatre; Bob Korzeniowski debate on admitting failures (Pandey: ask `Tell me about a production failure here and what changed after`).
