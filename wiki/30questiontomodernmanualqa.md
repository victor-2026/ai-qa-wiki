# AI‑Enhanced Manual QA – Interview Framework  

**Summary**  
Over the past three decades the role of a manual tester has been reshaped by large‑language models (LLMs) such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Copilot. Because virtually every candidate now uses an AI assistant, interviewers must probe *how* candidates think, validate, and govern AI‑generated artefacts rather than simply confirming tool usage. The following framework groups 30 AI‑centric questions into six thematic areas—critical thinking, risk analysis, requirements handling, decision making, productivity, leadership, and AI evaluation—providing a concise guide for assessing a tester’s ability to work responsibly with AI.

---

## Key Concepts  

| Theme | Core Idea | Representative Questions |
|-------|-----------|---------------------------|
| **AI & Critical Thinking** | Detecting hallucinations, knowing when AI output needs human review. | “When did AI give a confident but wrong answer? How did you spot it?” |
| **AI & Risk Analysis** | Ensuring AI‑generated tests cover real business risk, not just numeric coverage. | “How would you validate that 500 AI‑generated test cases are sufficient?” |
| **AI & Requirements** | Verifying AI’s interpretation of ambiguous or incomplete user stories. | “How do you confirm AI understood a vague requirement?” |
| **AI & Decision Making** | Balancing AI suggestions against experience and establishing confidence thresholds. | “If AI and your experience disagree, what do you do?” |
| **AI & Productivity** | Measuring quality gains versus speed, identifying tasks that remain human‑only. | “Has AI made you more effective or just faster? Give an example.” |
| **AI & Leadership** | Preventing over‑reliance, instituting governance, and measuring impact on team quality. | “What AI governance practices would you introduce?” |
| **AI Evaluation** | Testing the AI agent itself, defining metrics, spotting hallucinations, comparing models. | “Which metrics would you use to evaluate AI‑generated test cases?” |

*Additional concepts* – **hallucination detection**, **risk‑based test prioritisation**, **AI confidence calibration**, **governance & ethics**, **model comparison**, **continuous validation loops**.

## Practical Applications  

1. **Test‑case Review Pipeline**  
   - AI drafts a bulk set of cases → tester validates completeness, risk relevance, and business impact before acceptance.  
2. **Requirement Clarification**  
   - Prompt LLM with a user story, then manually audit ambiguous interpretations; use AI suggestions to surface missing requirements.  
3. **Prioritisation Matrix**  
   - Combine AI‑generated risk scores with domain knowledge to rank cases, ensuring high‑impact scenarios are tested first.  
4. **Metrics Dashboard**  
   - Track defect detection rate, false‑positive/negative ratios, and coverage gaps for AI‑produced artefacts.  
5. **Governance Checklist**  
   - Define “never‑accept‑without‑review” artefacts (e.g., security tests), set confidence thresholds, and schedule periodic AI model audits.  
6. **Training & Mentoring**  
   - Coach junior testers on prompt engineering and critical evaluation to avoid over‑dependence.
