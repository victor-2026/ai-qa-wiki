# AI Regression Testing Workflows
## Summary
AI is transforming regression testing workflows by addressing traditional pain points such as slow test runs, high test maintenance costs, and limited test coverage. By leveraging AI, teams can generate tests, prioritize high-risk tests, and detect flaky tests, resulting in faster and more efficient regression testing.

## Key Concepts
* **Test generation**: AI generates tests from requirements, logs, and code
* **Test prioritization**: AI prioritizes tests based on defect patterns and risk
* **Gap analysis**: AI identifies under-tested areas and fills gaps in test coverage
* **Script maintenance**: AI self-heals broken scripts and locators
* **Flakiness detection**: AI detects and quarantines unstable tests
* **Test data generation**: AI generates synthetic test data for edge cases

## Practical Applications
AI regression testing workflows can be applied in various scenarios, including:
* **In-house development**: Teams can develop and train their own AI models for regression testing
* **Partnering with AI vendors**: Teams can partner with AI vendors to leverage pre-built AI models and expertise
* **DevOps integration**: AI can be integrated into DevOps pipelines to provide quality signals and enable continuous testing
