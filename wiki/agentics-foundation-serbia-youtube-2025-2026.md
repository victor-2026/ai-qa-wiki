# Agentics Foundation Serbia — YouTube Channel

**Organizer:** Dragan Spiridonov (Head of Agentic QE, Cognitum One; Agentics Foundation Secretary & Ambassador for Serbia)
**Location:** StartIt AI Hub, Novi Sad, Serbia
**Cadence:** Bi-weekly meetups (Thu/Fri), recorded and published on YouTube
**Channel:** https://www.youtube.com/@AgenticsFoundationSerbia
**Global:** https://agentics.org | https://videos.agentics.org
**LinkedIn:** https://www.linkedin.com/in/dragan-spiridonov/

**What it is:** Longest-running in-person Agentics Foundation chapter globally (confirmed by Adam Kovacs, Meetup #14). Practitioner community building agentic QE tools in public — real demos, real failures, no polished presentations.

**Core tools built by the community:**
- **Agentic QE Fleet** — 13 QE domains, agent definitions, QE Queen orchestrator, hooks for deterministic execution
- **RuFlo** (formerly CloudFlow) — agent orchestration framework
- **Nagual-QE** — self-learning knowledge system (Rust, RuVector hyperbolic vector DB)
- **Vibium** — QE Browser skill (10MB, WebDriver BiDi, by Jason Huggins creator of Selenium)
- **Community Social Network** — the project they're building together across meetups
