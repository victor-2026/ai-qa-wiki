# SpaceDucking — "Test Feels Like Autopilot"

> Ministry of Testing (2026). Author: Sathish. Published: Sep 9, 2026.

Personal experience report: 8-month QA role where manual testing tasks were handled entirely by AI agents — 98% handoff rate. The tester's role became AI orchestration rather than manual execution.

## Core Claims
- "Test Feels Like Autopilot"
- "AI replaced 98% of my manual testing"
- "8 months in this role as a QA, where I use AI to handle most of my manual testing tasks"
- "I don't write my own manual testing and the AI does it for me"

## Pattern: Handoff Scale
- **Level 0**: No AI (fully manual)
- **Level 1**: AI assists (code completion, chat)
- **Level 2**: AI executes (agent runs manual scenarios) ← Sathish
- **Level 3**: AI plans + executes (autonomous test strategy)
- **Level 4**: AI plans + executes + self-heals (autonomous QA)

### Meta observations
- Different from "AI will replace testers" narrative — this is "AI replaces manual testing while tester becomes orchestrator"
- **The risk**: deep QA knowledge atrophies when you never do manual testing yourself

## Key Questions for QA
1. If AI handles 98% of manual testing, what does the QA actually test?
2. Who validates the AI's test results?
3. Does domain knowledge erode when you outsource exploration?
4. What happens when the AI confidently tests the wrong thing?
5. Is this scalable or does it create hidden quality debt?
